# Instagram ki apni automation (koi token ya third party nahi) - Ahmed bhai ke liye

Menu ke naam Instagram ke version ke hisaab se thode alag ho sakte hain. Sab kuch @hawa_taps_ ke Business/Creator account mein hi hota hai.

## Pehle
1. Password badlo, Two-factor ON karo (Settings > Accounts Center > Password and security).
2. Account type Business ya Creator ho (Settings > Account type and tools).

## Setup: Settings > Messages and story replies (ya Professional dashboard > Inbox)

### 1. Greeting message (naye customer ko apne aap)
Text:
Assalam-o-alaikum! Hawa Taps mein aapka swagat hai. Nal ke baare mein aapko kya jaanna hai? Neeche se sawaal chun lein ya likh dein, Ahmed bhai jaldi reply karenge.

### 2. Frequently asked questions (4 sawaal tak, customer tap karta hai to jawab apne aap jaata hai)
1. Sawaal: Nal ka daam kya hai?
   Jawab: Ek nal ki keemat ₹300 hai. Courier ke zariye All India delivery ho jaati hai, jahan chahiye wahan.
2. Sawaal: Aap kahan se hain?
   Jawab: Hamara head office Nagpada, Mumbai mein hai, factory Taloja, Mumbai mein hai. Maal courier ke zariye aap tak aa jaayega.
3. Sawaal: Warranty milti hai?
   Jawab: 1 saal warranty. Uske baad bhi kuch hua toh kharab nal Ahmad bhai tak bhej dena, badle mein naya nal free of cost mil jayega inshallah.
4. Sawaal: Nal kis material ka hai?
   Jawab: Nal ABS material ka bana hai, saade plastic se 10 guna zyada mazboot, aur button aluminium ka hai. Isse chhote models Amazon pe ₹350 aur ₹400 mein bikte hain, ye wale ki keemat ₹300 per nal hai.

### 3. Away message (jab aap busy ho)
Text:
Aapka message mil gaya. Ahmed bhai thodi der mein khud reply karenge. Shukriya!

### 4. Saved replies (aap khud jaldi jawab dene ke liye)
Shortcut likhne par poora jawab aa jaata hai. Ye apne aap nahi bhejta, aap ek tap se bhejte ho.
- `size` : Naal half inch size ka hai.
- `strong` : Humne 70kg ka aadmi naal pe khada kar diya, tab bhi naal nahi toota. Body ABS aur button aluminium ka hai.
- `origin` : Hamara maal Taloja MIDC factory area se aata hai. Courier se All India delivery hoti hai.
- `paani` : Ye taps 70-80% paani bachate hain, ek nal se roz kam-se-kam 60 litre ki bachat. 50,000+ masjids, 30,000+ madarse aur 1,000+ schools/hospitals mein use ho raha hai.
- `order` : Bahut badhiya! Kitne nal chahiye, apna naam, pura address aur pincode bhej dein. Courier se bhej denge.

## Kya hoga aur kya nahi
- Greeting, FAQ buttons aur away message apne aap chalte hain.
- Customer khud kuch likhe (jaise "size kya hai?") toh apne aap jawab nahi milega, aapko dena hoga (saved replies se ek tap mein).
- 3 ghante ka follow-up aur AI jawab isme nahi hain. Ye ManyChat ya humare Python bot mein hain.
