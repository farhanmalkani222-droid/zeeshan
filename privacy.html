Privacy Policy - Hawa Taps

Last updated: 2026-09-29. Hawa Taps (Instagram: @hawa_taps_, Nagpada, Mumbai) sells water-saving taps. This policy explains how we handle messages you send us on Instagram.

What we collect

When you message @hawa_taps_, we receive your Instagram-scoped user ID and the content of your messages (text, and a note when you send photos or voice notes). We do not receive your Instagram password.

How we use it

We use your messages only to answer your questions about our products, prices, delivery and orders, and to send one follow-up if the conversation goes quiet. Some replies are generated automatically. To write a reply to a question we cannot answer from our fixed answers, the recent messages in the conversation may be processed by an AI service (Anthropic). We do not use your messages for advertising and we do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Conversation history is stored on our server so the assistant can follow the chat. We keep it only as long as needed to serve you, and delete it on request.

Sharing

We do not share your messages with anyone except the service providers needed to run the assistant (Meta, our hosting provider, and Anthropic for AI-generated replies), or when the law requires it.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to delete your conversation data at any time by sending a message to @hawa_taps_ on Instagram saying "delete my data", and the owner will remove it. You can also ask to talk to the owner directly instead of the automated assistant.

Contact

Hawa Taps, Nagpada, Mumbai. Instagram: @hawa_taps_.
