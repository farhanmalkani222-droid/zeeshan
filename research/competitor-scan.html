India Kraft Fibre — Competitor & Prior-Art Scan

Has anyone in India already started this? Who is doing domestic-fibre substitution for kraft/packaging paper, how they differ from the proposed trading model, and where the open niche is.

Prepared 2 Oct 2026 · Companion to the import-substitution research & mill-trial protocol · Threat ratings are this analysis's assessment; prices/figures are indicative public data — verify directly.

VERDICT IN ONE LINE: The idea (use Indian fibre instead of US/EU imports) is already active in India — several mills and research bodies pursue it. But the specific business model you described — a trader who stocks and sells a drop-in domestic long-fibre + strength-chemistry blend to the many small recycled-kraft mills, per-tonne gain-share, no machine change — does not appear to be occupied. You are not first to the idea; you may be first to the model.

4 · Why the niche is open — idea vs model

What's TAKEN

  • Integrated agro/bamboo mills making their own finished paper (Yash Pakka, Ruchira, KR)
  • Chemical majors selling dry-strength to large mills
  • Government/research pushing bamboo & agro substitution

What's OPEN (your slot)

  • A trader/"arms dealer" — not a mill
  • Sells a drop-in blend (domestic long fibre + chemistry) to the hundreds of small recycled-kraft mills
  • Warehouse-stocked, daily-priced, no shipment lag
  • Per-tonne gain-share, no machine change at the mill
  • Serves the under-serviced 16–28 BF Gujarat/UP/Punjab segment the majors ignore

5 · Scout before you scale (do this first)

  • Ask 5–10 mills (your 50 associated) exactly what fibre & chemistry they buy today, and from whom.
  • Ask the chemical/enzyme suppliers (Solenis/Kemira/Ecolab/Buckman, Advanced Enzymes) who else is trialling domestic-fibre strength programs in your belt.
  • Check local traders in Morbi/Vapi/Ahmedabad for anyone already blending/stocking domestic long fibre.
  • Watch the bamboo DPRs — if a 20–40k MT bamboo pulp plant comes online near you, that's a supply source and a potential competitor.
  • Protect the model early: patent your specific recipe/application method; lock the first mills on gain-share before the segment is noticed.
Honest caveat: absence of public evidence is not proof nobody is doing this privately. This scan reflects publicly available information as of Oct 2026. Threat ratings are judgement calls, and company figures are indicative public data — confirm by direct scouting and supplier quotes before committing capital.
